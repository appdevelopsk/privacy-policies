1. Information We Collect

LifeTrace collects the following data to provide its AI life recording functionality:

  • Apple Health Data (with your permission): Steps, distance, active energy burned, exercise time, and sleep data via HealthKit. This data is used solely to generate your daily AI life log.
  • Location Data (with your permission): Approximate location when generating a life log, to provide context for your daily record.
  • Journal entries and mood logs you create within the app.
  • Usage analytics via Firebase Analytics (anonymized).

2. Apple Health Integration

LifeTrace integrates with Apple HealthKit to read activity and wellness data including steps taken, distance walked, calories burned, exercise minutes, and sleep duration. We request Read-only access to this data.

We never write to HealthKit, and your Health data is never sold or shared with third parties. Health data is processed solely to generate your personalized AI life summary.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate your AI-powered daily life log and insights
  • To display activity statistics within the app
  • To provide personalized wellness insights on the Insights screen
  • To send optional daily reminder notifications (if you enable them)

4. Data Storage and Security

Your journal entries and life logs are stored securely on our servers. We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage. Health data is processed transiently and is not permanently stored on our servers without your explicit journal creation action.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share anonymized, aggregated usage statistics with analytics providers. Your Health data is never shared with any third party.

6. Advertising

LifeTrace displays advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings, or purchase the ad-free experience in the app.

7. App Tracking Transparency

In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, we request your permission before tracking your data for advertising purposes. You can change your tracking preference at any time in Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

8. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at app.develop.sk@gmail.com. Health data access can be revoked at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

9. Children's Privacy

LifeTrace is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
