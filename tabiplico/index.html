Tabiplico – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Tabiplico ("the App") handles information. The App has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not operate a server for the App and we do not collect personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

  • The trips ("booklets") you save, including any notes, meeting place, reservations and transport you type in.
  • Your settings, such as the display language and preferred ways of getting around.

This information stays on your device. It is removed when you delete a booklet, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

2. Information sent to third-party services

To find real places, the App contacts the following services directly from your device. As with any internet request, these services receive your IP address.

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) – the place name you type, when you press the search button, and your display language. Privacy policy
  • Overpass API servers (overpass-api.de, maps.mail.ru, overpass.private.coffee – tried in this order until one answers) – the coordinates of the destination you chose and a search radius, to list nearby places.
  • Google Fonts – when you create a PDF in Chinese, Korean, Thai, Arabic or Turkish, the App downloads a font file. No trip content is sent. Privacy policy
  • openstreetmap.org – only if you tap a link to open a place on the map; it opens in your browser.

The App does not request or use your device location. Your saved trips, notes and budget are never sent anywhere. In sample mode the App makes no place requests at all.

3. Printing and sharing

When you print or share a PDF booklet, the file is handed to the printing or sharing feature of your device and to the app you choose. That handling is governed by those apps.

4. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything, delete your booklets in the App or uninstall it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest revision.
