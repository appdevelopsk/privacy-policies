AstroTrace - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AstroTrace ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application AstroTrace ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App stores your preferences (favorites, notes, purchased packs) locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob - for displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics - for anonymous usage analytics. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Sentry - for crash reporting. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchase - for premium content purchases. Transactions are handled by Apple.

3. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data on our servers. All app data is stored locally on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: appdevelopsk@gmail.com
