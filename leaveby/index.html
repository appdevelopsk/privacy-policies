LeaveBy - Departure Timer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Key Point: LeaveBy has no server and no account. Every departure plan you create — arrival times, travel times and prep steps — is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded to us.

1. Information We Collect

LeaveBy does not operate a backend. The app stores the following on your device only, using the operating system's local storage:

  • Departure plans — plan names, icons, arrival times, travel and buffer minutes, and your prep steps
  • Purchase state — whether the ad-free upgrade has been purchased

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, precise location, or the contents of your plans. Nothing you type into LeaveBy leaves your device unless you explicitly export it and share it yourself.

2. Third-Party Services

The app includes the following third-party SDKs, each of which may process data as described in its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — displays banner advertising in the free version. AdMob may use a device advertising identifier. See Google's advertising policy. Ads are removed entirely once the in-app purchase is made.
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous, aggregated usage statistics such as which screens are opened. See Firebase privacy.
  • Sentry — crash and error reports, so we can fix bugs. Reports contain diagnostic data such as the error, device model and OS version. See Sentry's privacy policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchase — processes the ad-free upgrade. We never receive or store your payment details.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, LeaveBy asks for your permission before any tracking identifier is used for advertising. If you decline, advertising continues in a non-personalised form. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Notifications

If you enable the optional reminder, LeaveBy schedules a local notification on your device at the time your preparation must begin. Local notifications are generated entirely on the device and are not sent through any server.

5. Export and Import

You can export your plans as a JSON backup. The export is handed to the iOS or Android share sheet, and you choose where it goes. We never receive a copy. Imported files are read on the device and replace your local plans.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your plans never leave your device, deleting the app deletes all of them. There is no account to close and nothing for us to erase on your behalf. Analytics and crash data held by the providers above are retained according to their own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

LeaveBy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Advertising is configured to serve only content rated suitable for general audiences.

8. Security

All network requests made by the third-party SDKs listed above use HTTPS. No payment information is collected or stored by the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
