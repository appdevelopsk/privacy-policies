DatePilot - Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 4, 2026

DatePilot ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to ensuring the safety of all users, particularly minors. This document outlines our standards and practices regarding the prevention of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Age Requirement

DatePilot is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow minors to use the app. Users must confirm they are at least 18 years old before accessing the app's features.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE

We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content, behavior, or activity that exploits or endangers minors is strictly prohibited, including but not limited to:

  • Sharing, distributing, or soliciting child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Grooming or attempting to engage minors in inappropriate conversations
  • Any form of sexual exploitation or abuse of minors
  • Promoting or glorifying abuse of minors

3. Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report any suspected CSAE content or behavior through the following channels:

  • In-app reporting: Use the report function available on user profiles and content
  • Email: appdevelopsk@gmail.com — Reports are reviewed within 24 hours

We also report confirmed CSAE incidents to relevant law enforcement authorities and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by law.

4. Content Moderation

We employ the following measures to detect and prevent CSAE:

  • AI-powered content moderation to detect inappropriate content
  • User reporting systems with prompt review processes
  • Immediate account suspension upon detection of CSAE-related activity
  • Permanent ban for confirmed violations with no possibility of reinstatement

5. Data Handling for Safety

When CSAE is suspected or confirmed, we may retain relevant data as required by law for reporting to authorities. This data is handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our Privacy Policy.

6. Contact Information

For questions or concerns regarding our child safety standards, please contact us:

  • Email: appdevelopsk@gmail.com
  • We typically respond within 1–2 business days
