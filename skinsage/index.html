Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI skin analysis work?

SkinSage uses computer vision AI to analyze facial photos and identify skin type, concerns, and personalized recommendations. The analysis is for general wellness guidance only and is not a medical diagnosis.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time through Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Is my photo data stored?

Photos are processed on our servers solely for analysis and are not retained. See our privacy policy below for details.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account within the app, or email us at app.develop.sk@gmail.com.
