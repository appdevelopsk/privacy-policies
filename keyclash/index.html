About KeyClash: Typing Battle

Type faster than anyone — prove it in real-time. KeyClash is the ultimate real-time typing battle. Race against players worldwide and see who can type the fastest.

FAQ

Q: How does matchmaking work?
A: KeyClash matches you with players of similar skill level. If no match is found within 8 seconds, you'll play against a bot opponent.

Q: What languages are supported?
A: KeyClash supports 17 languages for typing practice including English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and more.

Q: How is WPM calculated?
A: Words Per Minute is calculated as (correct characters / 5) / elapsed minutes.
