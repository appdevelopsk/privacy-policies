Melt - Child Safety Standards

Our commitment to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Age Requirement

Melt is designed exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. Users must confirm their age during registration. Accounts belonging to minors are immediately terminated upon detection.

Zero Tolerance Policy

Melt maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child exploitation. Any content or behavior that exploits or endangers minors is strictly prohibited and will result in:

  • Immediate account termination
  • Preservation of evidence for law enforcement
  • Reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant local authorities

Detection and Prevention

  • We use automated systems to detect and prevent the sharing of CSAM
  • All user-generated content is subject to moderation
  • We employ age verification measures during account creation
  • Suspected underage users are promptly investigated and removed

In-App Reporting

Users can report child safety concerns directly within the app through the reporting feature available on every profile and in chat. Reports related to child safety are treated with the highest priority and reviewed immediately.

Cooperation with Authorities

Melt cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies worldwide. We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including mandatory reporting obligations to national and regional authorities.
