About TaleTime Tales

TaleTime Tales is a magical co-creation game where parents and children build imaginative stories together with the help of AI. Take turns adding to the story while an AI storyteller weaves in creative, age-appropriate content between each turn.

Features

  • Take turns adding to the story — parents and children alternate
  • AI storyteller fills in the adventure with creative content
  • Choose from 10 themes: fantasy, space, ocean, dinosaurs, and more
  • Save complete stories as family memories
  • Multiple child profiles supported
  • 17 languages supported
