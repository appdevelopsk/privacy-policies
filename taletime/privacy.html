TaleTime Tales

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Family-friendly: TaleTime Tales is designed for parents and children to create stories together. Story content is processed by AI for the sole purpose of generating the story, and is not used for any other purpose.

1. Information We Collect

TaleTime Tales collects the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Story text and choices — what you and your child write to build the story
  • Child profile data — nicknames and age range you optionally enter for each child profile, stored only on your device
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active premium subscription, validated through Apple/Google's in-app purchase systems
  • Device information — device type, OS version, and language preference for app functionality

We do not collect: your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, or any directly identifying information about you or your child.

2. AI Story Generation

When you and your child take turns building a story, the story text you submit is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to generate the next story segment. The AI uses only the story text you provide — it does not receive identifying information about you or your child.

Anthropic's data processing is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy. Anthropic does not use your story content to train AI models.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate AI story content during co-creation sessions
  • Save completed stories to your device for replay
  • Manage premium subscription status
  • Provide app crash diagnostics through Sentry (anonymized, no personal data)

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

TaleTime Tales is intended for use by parents with their children. The app is designed assuming a parent is present and supervising. Child profiles store only a nickname and age range that the parent enters; no other information about the child is requested or collected.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us and we will promptly delete the information.

5. In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Payment information is handled entirely by these platforms — TaleTime Tales never receives or stores your payment details.

Premium is available as a monthly ($2.99/month) or yearly ($19.99/year) auto-renewable subscription. Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage subscriptions in your App Store / Google Play account settings.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Story content, child profiles, and saved stories are stored locally on your device. They are not synced to any cloud service unless you explicitly choose to back them up via your device's standard backup (iCloud, Google Backup).

You can delete all stored data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the in-app reset option.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude API — AI story generation (text-only, no identifying info)
  • Apple / Google in-app purchases — subscription management
  • Sentry — anonymous crash diagnostics
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics for app improvement

8. Security

We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission. Story content is transmitted only to Anthropic's servers for the purpose of AI generation. No payment information is collected or stored by TaleTime Tales.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
