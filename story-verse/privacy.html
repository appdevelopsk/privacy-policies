Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Privacy-first: StoryVerse is designed to respect your privacy. We collect only what's necessary to provide the service.

1. Information We Collect

StoryVerse may collect the following information:

  • Device information — device type, OS version, language preference for app functionality
  • Anonymous usage data — feature usage statistics for app improvement (Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash diagnostics — anonymized crash reports (Sentry)
  • Subscription status — if you have an active premium subscription, validated through Apple/Google's in-app purchase systems

We do not collect: your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, or any directly identifying personal information unless you explicitly provide it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide app functionality and features
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the app
  • Diagnose and fix crashes
  • Manage premium subscription status

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. In-App Purchases

If StoryVerse offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Payment information is handled entirely by these platforms — StoryVerse never receives or stores your payment details.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics
  • Sentry — anonymous crash diagnostics
  • Apple / Google in-app purchases — subscription management (where applicable)
  • AdMob — advertising (where applicable; you can opt out via OS-level App Tracking Transparency settings)

5. Data Storage and Retention

App data (preferences, history, etc.) is stored locally on your device. It is not synced to any cloud service unless you explicitly choose to back it up via your device's standard backup (iCloud, Google Backup).

You can delete all stored data at any time by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

StoryVerse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us and we will promptly delete the information.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission. We use industry-standard practices to protect data. No payment information is collected or stored by StoryVerse.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
