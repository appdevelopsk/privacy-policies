About HeritageTrace

HeritageTrace is a world heritage sites encyclopedia featuring ancient ruins, natural wonders, historic cities, and cultural landscapes from around the globe. Learn about world heritage through daily cards, trace identification, side-by-side comparisons, and quizzes. Keep research notes and unlock premium content packs.
