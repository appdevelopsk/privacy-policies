About TwoTruths: 2 Truths 1 Lie

TwoTruths brings the classic party game "2 Truths and 1 Lie" to your phone! Post three statements about yourself — two true, one false — and challenge others to guess which one is the lie. HOW TO PLAY: - Write two true statements and one lie about yourself - Post them for the community to see - Other players vote on which statement they think is the lie - See how many people you fooled - Guess on other players' posts and climb the leaderboard
