Privacy Policy — Excel Formula Helper

Last updated: April 2026

Information We Collect

Excel Formula Helper collects anonymous usage analytics (screen views, feature interactions) via Firebase Analytics to improve the app experience. No personally identifiable information is collected or stored.

Camera Usage

Camera access is used exclusively for the OCR Scanner feature, which allows you to photograph spreadsheets or handwritten notes to extract formula text. Images are processed on-device using Google ML Kit and are never uploaded to any server or stored beyond the current session.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization, subject to your device's tracking preferences and App Tracking Transparency consent.

Data Storage

Formula history and favorites are stored locally on your device only and are not transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC)
  • Google ML Kit — on-device OCR (Google LLC)
  • Sentry (error monitoring)
